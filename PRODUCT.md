# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Misael Lima is the initial and primary user. He uses the product as an ongoing personal workspace to understand what to learn next, record evidence of learning, and see how far he has progressed toward becoming a well-rounded software engineer.

## Product Purpose

Engineering Compass turns a broad software-engineering roadmap into an observable learning journey. Success means the user can look at the product, understand current direction and momentum, find the next meaningful step, and support a future judgment that enough breadth and depth have been learned.

## Positioning

The product connects roadmap, progress, and evidence in one personal system: a capability is not merely checked off, but can eventually be supported by notes, projects, practice, or other proof of learning.

## Operating Context

The product will live at `skills.misaellima.com` and be revisited repeatedly during study, practice, and reflection. The first surface is a dashboard-oriented application with navigation, progress summaries, roadmap visibility, and information-dense views.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current repository is a Next.js scaffold.
- The initial milestone is inferred as a navigable, responsive dashboard foundation with representative data; real persistence and account infrastructure remain open decisions.
- Visual foundations must be easy to change locally through semantic tokens and configuration.
- The product must not import styling or runtime code from the portfolio repository.
- The interface language for the initial milestone is Portuguese (Brazil), inferred from the brief; future localization remains open.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Engineering Compass.
- It belongs to the same ecosystem as the portfolio at `misaellima.com`.
- It inherits the portfolio's fundamental color, typography, and personality cues, while establishing an application-specific identity suited to navigation, progress, and dense information.
- Future changes to the portfolio must not require rebuilding this product; shared cues are copied into an independently owned token layer.

## Evidence on Hand

- Portfolio implementation in the sibling `misael-lima` repository is the current source of brand evidence.
- No production roadmap content, learning records, benchmarks, testimonials, or completion criteria have been provided; the initial UI must label representative content as demonstrative where confusion is possible.

## Product Principles

1. Direction before volume: make the next meaningful learning step clear.
2. Evidence over checkbox theater: progress should become explainable, not merely countable.
3. Calm density: support substantial information without making study feel like administration.
4. Independent kinship: feel related to the portfolio while remaining self-contained and application-native.
5. Growth without rewrites: tokens, navigation, and domain structures should support gradual expansion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience should preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion preferences, semantic landmarks, and readable contrast from the first milestone.
