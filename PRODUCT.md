## Platform

web

## Purpose

Engineering Compass is a personal platform for following a software engineering roadmap and tracking progress. It will be published at `skills.misaellima.com`.

The rules live in [Standard v1.1](docs/standard/standard-v1.1.md): 24 domains, 455 leveled competencies, 27 experiences, and 8 Depth Gates. The current level is always derived, never edited by hand.

## Current Scope

Single-user MVP described in [docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md): assess competencies, experiences, and depth criteria; derive L1–L4; keep history; export data.

## Brand

It shares colors, typography, and visual personality with `misaellima.com` while keeping its tokens and implementation independent.
